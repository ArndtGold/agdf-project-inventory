# Findings And Gaps Register Structure

Every statement records one class, one claim type, one support state, its claimed evidence lane,
resolvable evidence references, impact and review state. A missing unknown also records whether the
condition was not checked, inaccessible or insufficiently evidenced.

Allowed classes are observation, interpretation, unknown and recommendation. Allowed support states
are supported, partial, conflicting and missing. Do not include a prewritten finding, rating or
recommendation.

Absence of evidence, inaccessible evidence, evidence of absence and conflicting evidence remain
separate derived states. Do not use inaccessible evidence to support an observation, interpretation
or recommendation.
